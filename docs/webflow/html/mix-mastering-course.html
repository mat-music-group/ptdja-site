FAQ

Is this course suitable for self-releasing artists?

Yes. It's designed to help you prepare your tracks for platforms like Spotify, Beatport, and more.

What topics are covered in mixing and mastering?

EQ, compression, stereo imaging, reverb, loudness, dynamics, exporting and more.

Will I be able to finalize tracks for streaming?

Yes — after the course you’ll know how to prepare your mix/master for release.

Can I get feedback on my mix?

Yes. Through live sessions and Talent Pool submissions.

Is mastering included in all subscription plans?

No — the Mixing & Mastering course is only available in the Elite (3-course) plan. It’s an advanced course designed for students who want full access to the entire learning path.

Do I need the full access plan to get everything?

All subscription plans include the same features: live sessions, AI tutors, contests, feedback, downloads, and community access.

However, to unlock the Mixing & Mastering course and its exclusive content, you’ll need the Elite plan (3-course access) — which gives you the full educational experience from DJing to final mastering.

Is certification included in the price?

Yes. Complete the course and you'll receive an official certificate.
