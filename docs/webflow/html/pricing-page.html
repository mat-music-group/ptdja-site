FAQ

What’s the difference between the plans?

Each plan gives you access to 1, 2, or all 3 courses — the more you unlock, the more content and opportunities you get.

What’s included in every subscription?

AI tutors, live sessions, downloadable tools, contests, certificates, label access, and the Talent Pool.

Can I access everything with the 3-course plan?

Yes. It's the full-access plan — everything unlocked, no limits.

Will I lose progress if I downgrade?

No, your progress is saved. You’ll just lose access to the courses you remove.

How much does it cost to join?

Plans start from 13/month — pricing depends on how many courses you include.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, anytime. No lock-ins or penalties.

Are there discounts for longer plans?

Yes — yearly plans come with reduced monthly pricing.
